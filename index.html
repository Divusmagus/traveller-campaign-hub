Patch Notes

v16.1.4 — System name generator fix

Restored automatic naming for Random World and added a Generate Name button for rerolling independently.

Release history, newest first. Earlier versions are listed only where changes are documented.

v16.1.4 · Payroll reset and in-universe invoice dates

GM-only confirmed testing reset clears invoices and accrued payroll, returns baseline to day zero, and displays in-universe dates prominently on invoices with real-world timestamps in fine print. Existing personal credits remain unchanged.

v16.1.2 · Payroll accrual fix

Fixes missing payroll baseline, accrual during calendar advances and pay-rate changes, and displays the tracked payroll day.

v16.1.1 · Military salary, mission bonuses, fuel scooping and linked species randomization

Shared per-player salary and completed-mission bonuses, crew payouts, fuel-scooping sessions and generated species selections. Requires v16.1 Supabase migration.

v16.0.2 · Overview layout correction

Active Missions now sits below all five ship summary indicators. Removed the redundant fuel and cargo explanation from Ship Overview.

v16.0.2 · Cargo and overview layout refinements

Personal credit balances are always visible in player lockers; refueling is integrated into the fuel tank; the compact ship treasury is moved beside storage; overview gauges are grouped above food and treasury indicators.

v16.0.0 · Ship economy and automated commerce

Shared ship treasury and personal credit balances, crew payouts, configurable fuel pricing and purchases, world sale estimates, server-rolled trade quotes, and individual or bulk cargo sales with GM permissions. Requires the v16 Supabase economy migration.

v15.5.4 · Hull adjustment correction

Hull integrity accepts single-point changes; ship repairs use 10-point increments. Server-side journey rules updated with a matching Supabase migration.

v15.5.3 · Storage, travel and star-system organization

Consolidated cargo and fuel gauges, corrected Jump rating refresh, hull adjustment increments, star-system folders and shared-draft diagnostics.

v15.5.2 · Ship systems & quality-of-life

Unified Jump rating, improved travel layout, default study advancement, hull instruments and controls, ship repairs under Cargo, star-name generation, GM tab styling, navigation cleanup and public patch notes.

v15.5.1 · Trade policy

Accord-wide and species-specific legal trade policy, with inherited laws and exceptions.

v15.5.0 · Commerce & world generation

Trade & Commerce estimates, diplomatic disposition, world classifications, and GM world-generation controls.

Earlier releases predate this patch-notes record; no undocumented changes have been reconstructed.
